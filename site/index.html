Everything you copy, kept close.

Copyhold is a clipboard manager for Chrome, Edge, and Firefox. It keeps the text and images you copy, finds them again in a keystroke, masks secrets, and never sends a single clip off your device.

Built for the way you actually copy and paste

Open it, type a few letters, press Enter, and the popup gets out of your way.

Keyboard first

Open with Alt+Shift+Y, search, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter to copy. Alt+1 to 9 copy by position.

Saves as you copy, if you want

Turn on automatic saving and Copyhold keeps what you copy, even with the popup closed. Copy something again and it moves to the top instead of repeating.

Private by design

No servers, no accounts, no analytics, no network requests. Your clips live in your browser profile and nowhere else.

Passphrase lock

Encrypt every clip with a passphrase. Copyhold keeps saving while locked and locks again when you step away or lock your computer.

Secrets stay out of sight

Passwords, API keys, and card numbers are spotted on your device and masked automatically. Hide every preview with one click while you share your screen.

Categories, tags, and rules

File clips under colour-coded categories, add tags, and filter by them. Rules organize new clips for you, and #tag in search finds tagged clips.

Templates and Copy as

Templates fill in the date, time, or your clipboard when copied. Copy any clip trimmed, in another case, or as formatted JSON.

Command palette

Press ⌘+K or Ctrl+K to run any action or jump to any category or tag by typing.

Images too

Save screenshots and copied images with a thumbnail, and copy them back as images.

Save without opening it

Press Alt+Shift+S to save the clipboard, or right-click selected text and choose Save selection to Copyhold.

Clips that tidy themselves

Set a clip to delete itself after an hour, a day, or a week, give new clips a default retention period, or cap your history size.

Fast with big histories

Opens and searches instantly with thousands of clips. Links, code, emails, and colors are recognized at a glance.

Compact view

Fit twice as many clips with one line each, showing how long ago you used it and how long it is.

Bulk actions

Select all, then copy several clips at once, pin them, or clear them out. Deleting one clip can always be undone.

A closer look

Light and dark themes follow your system, or pick one in Settings.

Install in under a minute

Copyhold is on its way to the Chrome Web Store. Until then, load it straight from the download. It works in Chrome and Microsoft Edge; a Firefox package is on each release.

  1. Download and unzip

    Download copyhold-chromium.zip and unzip it. Keep the copyhold folder somewhere it won't be deleted, because Chrome loads the extension from it.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and switch on Developer mode in the corner.

  3. Load the folder

    Select Load unpacked and choose the copyhold folder.

  4. Pin it and start copying

    Pin Copyhold from the puzzle-piece menu, or press Alt+Shift+Y (Option+Shift+Y on a Mac).

Updating: download the latest release, replace the files in your copyhold folder, and select the reload button on Copyhold's card in chrome://extensions. Your clips are kept. All versions are on the releases page.
