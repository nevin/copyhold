Everything you copy, kept close.

Copyhold is a clipboard manager for Chrome and Edge. It keeps the text you copy, lets you find it again in a keystroke, and never sends a single clip off your device.

Download for Chrome How to install

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store. Until then, install it from the download in under a minute.

Built for the way you actually copy and paste

Open it, type a few letters, press Enter, and the popup gets out of your way.

Keyboard first

Open with Alt+Shift+Y, search, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter to copy. Alt+1 to 9 copy by position.

Saves as you copy, if you want

Turn on automatic saving and Copyhold keeps what you copy, even with the popup closed. It's off until you choose, and you can pause it anytime.

Private by design

No servers, no accounts, no analytics, no network requests. Your clips live in your browser profile and nowhere else.

Sensitive text stays out of sight

Mask or hide a clip's preview so keys and passwords don't show on screen, and still copy the original with one click.

Clips that tidy themselves

Set a clip to delete itself after an hour, a day, or a week, give new clips a default retention period, or cap your history size.

Fast with big histories

Search stays instant with thousands of clips. Links, code, emails, and colors are recognized at a glance.

A closer look

Light and dark themes follow your system, or pick one in Settings.

Install in under a minute

Copyhold is on its way to the Chrome Web Store. Until then, load it straight from the download. It works in Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

  1. Download and unzip

    Download copyhold-chromium.zip and unzip it. Keep the copyhold folder somewhere it won't be deleted, because Chrome loads the extension from it.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and switch on Developer mode in the corner.

  3. Load the folder

    Select Load unpacked and choose the copyhold folder.

  4. Pin it and start copying

    Pin Copyhold from the puzzle-piece menu, or press Alt+Shift+Y (Option+Shift+Y on a Mac).

Updating: download the latest release, replace the files in your copyhold folder, and select the reload button on Copyhold's card in chrome://extensions. Your clips are kept. All versions are on the releases page.
